READ ALTERNATION CAREFULLY BEFORE COMPARING IT TO GEN 15. Gen 15's 0.736 was
measured on the Step lesson at a commanded 0.47 m/s. This run ended on Sway at
0.10 m/s, where the fighters are barely asked to travel and so have little
stepping to alternate. The two numbers are not comparable and 0.173 is NOT
evidence that this brain hops or stands. It is evidence that it never got asked
to walk properly, because the run spent 9M of its 18M budget on StandStill.

WHAT THIS RUN PROVED. Gen 16 ran the same three-body scene and plateaued at
0.18, which read as "one policy cannot drive three bodies". That was a bug, not
a result: Reward_Locomotion resolved the foot collider with
GetComponent<Collider>(), which is null for Grandma and Grandpa (their sensor
sits on the foot BONE, the collider on a child "_Sole" object), and the
clearance term dereferenced it every FixedUpdate. It threw AFTER the stats were
recorded and BEFORE AddReward, so ten of sixteen agents posted normal-looking
statistics while earning exactly zero. Fixed in
Reward_Locomotion.ResolveFootCollider.

With that fixed the three bodies CONVERGED: the capsule-to-Grandma reward ratio
closed from 4.4x at 1M to 1.41x at 6M, and steps-to-first-fall ended comparable
across all three. The cost of generality is a SLOWER CLIMB, not a lower ceiling
(9.0M vs 5.7M for the same gate).
