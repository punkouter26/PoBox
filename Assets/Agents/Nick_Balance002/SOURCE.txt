nick_balance_002.onnx
=====================

WHAT      Locomotion brain for Nick (RIGGED_Nick.glb on the MuJoCo Unity plugin),
          trained in MUJOCO WARP -- the second policy in this project not trained
          by Unity ML-Agents, and the comparison point for the Isaac Lab line.
CONTRACT  127 observations / 30 continuous actions.
          CreatureSentisController.GatherObservations with _observeLocomotionCommand
          on: the 121-term balance vector plus commanded speed, pelvis-local
          commanded direction (3) and the 1.4 Hz gait clock (sin, cos).
          Foot contact is rest-relative (ankle within 0.03 m of rest).
          Actions are zero-centred position targets in canonical order.
          Control every 1 physics step(s) of 0.0200 s (decimation 1, 50 Hz).
          EVALUATE AND RUN IT AT THIS STEP. A policy measured at another
          one measures a creature that is not this one.
          The observation normaliser is BAKED INTO THE GRAPH; feed raw.
          input obs_0 [batch,127], output continuous_actions [batch,30].
MODEL     Tools/MuJoCo/nick_unity.xml -- the MJCF MjScene generates, exported by
          RigTool_NickMuJoCo.ExportNickMjcf. NOT the authored creature.xml.
TRAINED   2026-09-09, results/nick/nick12, model_3700.pt (iteration 3700), rsl_rl PPO on mujoco_warp.
          Config: results/nick/nick12/config.json.
MEASURED  10 consecutive evaluations, 512 perturbed starts each, at its own 0.02 s x decimation 1 (eval_nick.py --repeats 10).
          RING (shoves + one ring hazard per world) 96.2% full-cap, min 95, max 98 -- the incumbent nick08 measured 76%.
          BALANCE (150 N every 4 s, 30 s) 97.9%, min 97 -- nick08 measured 84%.
          WALK (1 m/s, 20 s, clean) 86.5%, min 83, at 0.895 m/s, alternation 1.000 -- nick08 measured 64%.
          Effort and stability: torso tilt 6.5 deg standing / 9.4 walking, delivered actuator |f| 33.9 / 52.2 Nm, hinge |qacc| 96 / 150.
          Better than the brain it replaces on all three tables. See rl_optimization_log.md.
EXPORT    Tools/MuJoCo/export_onnx.py --run nick12 --checkpoint model_3700.pt
