nick_getup_001.onnx
===================

WHAT      Locomotion brain for Nick (RIGGED_Nick.glb on the MuJoCo Unity plugin),
          trained in MUJOCO WARP.
CONTRACT  127 observations / 30 continuous actions.
          CreatureSentisController.GatherObservations with _observeLocomotionCommand
          on: the 121-term balance vector plus commanded speed, pelvis-local
          commanded direction (3) and the 1.4 Hz gait clock (sin, cos).
          Foot contact is rest-relative (ankle within 0.03 m of rest).
          Actions are zero-centred position targets in canonical order.
          Control every 4 physics step(s) of 0.0050 s (decimation 4, 50 Hz).
          EVALUATE AND RUN IT AT THIS STEP. A policy measured at another
          one measures a creature that is not this one.
          The observation normaliser is BAKED INTO THE GRAPH; feed raw.
          input obs_0 [batch,127], output continuous_actions [batch,30].
MODEL     Tools/MuJoCo/nick_unity.xml -- the MJCF MjScene generates, exported from Unity.
          NOT the authored creature file.
TRAINED   2026-09-18, results/nick/nickgetup01, model_29999.pt (iteration 29999), rsl_rl PPO on mujoco_warp.
          Config: results/nick/nickgetup01/config.json.
MEASURED  Best of Nick get-up line 2026-09-18: 6% get-up success at the 4-second standard over 512 fresh fallen starts, zero relapses. Trained at timestep 0.005 x decimation 4; body nick_unity.xml. EVALUATED at NICK_TIMESTEP=0.005 NICK_DECIMATION=4. Regression: BAL 75% / WALK 74% (14.39 m, 0.70 m/s) / RING 82%. Previous best Nick_Torque002 was 0% on the same test.
EXPORT    Tools/MuJoCo/export_onnx.py --run nickgetup01 --checkpoint model_29999.pt
