RULED OUT before blaming the policy: that the mixed scene was mis-wired.
RigTool_LocomotionScene assigns fall sensors by hard-coded joint index (3 and 9
= shins), chosen against the capsule. All three prefabs order their joints
identically (3 = ShinL/LeftLeg, 9 = ShinR/RightLeg) and the foot sensors resolve
correctly, so the result is real rather than an artefact.

WHAT IT IS USEFUL FOR: nothing in the game. Do not wire this into a contest
scene. It exists to be run in inference in SCN_TRAIN_LOCOMOTION so the per-body
reward split can be read, which is the thing the trainer's 16-agent average
hides and the thing that decides what gen 17 should be.
