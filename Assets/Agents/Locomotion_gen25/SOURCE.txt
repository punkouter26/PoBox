IT WINS ON EVERY BODY. The standing rule in this project is that improving the
characters by wrecking the capsule is not shippable; gen 25 does not make that
trade in either direction.

UNDISTURBED, at commanded speed 0, it effectively stops falling: steps between
falls saturate the 3000-step episode on the capsule (2889) and Grandpa (2754)
during training, against gen 20's 80 on the capsule.

WHAT MADE THE DIFFERENCE, in the order the evidence arrived:

  1. THE GROUND SENSOR. Contact was a running Enter-minus-Exit tally that
     desynchronises on the character rigs and never recovers. Grandma's soles
     were four to eight millimetres BELOW the floor plane while both sensors
     reported airborne, so ten of sixteen fighters trained against a corrupted
     objective and read a corrupted observation vector. Re-affirming contact
     from OnCollisionStay every step fixed it, and improved gen 20 itself by 28%
     on Grandma with no retraining at all.

  2. THE STANCE THE REWARD COULD NOT SEE. At gaitBlend 0 the support and
     clearance terms both saturate whether the fighter is planted or holding one
     foot in the air, so a speed-0 run optimised neither. Gen 20 stands on one
     leg with the other 0.159 m up for 76% of an episode; the heuristic PD bot,
     which cannot, OUT-STOOD it on the capsule by 40%. Gen 25 reads
     singleSupport 0.000 on every body -- squarely on two feet.

  3. SIDEWAYS. The speed term scored only the component of pelvis velocity along
     the goal direction, so a fighter sliding sideways at 2 m/s scored a perfect
     1.0 on the heaviest term in the function. Sideways is the direction
     fighters fall.

  4. THE HORIZON. gamma 0.99 -> 0.995, lambd 0.95 -> 0.97, time_horizon 256 ->
     1000. At lambd 0.95 advantages stop propagating after 0.4 s, and a fall is
     caused by a posture entered one to two seconds earlier.
