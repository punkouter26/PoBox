boxer_locomotion12, final model (step 18,000,102). Full 18M-step run.

THE FIRST BRAIN IN THIS PROJECT THAT ACTUALLY STEPS, and the first to get past
the Shuffle rung. It is NOT contest-usable: it falls roughly every 84 steps
(1.7 s). Kept for provenance and as the starting point of the walking line.

  SingleSupportMean  0.261 at the end, still climbing
  ClearanceMean      0.379 (true ground clearance, ~1.9 cm)
  1st fall           84 steps at the end; peak 1,571 steps (31.4 s) at 7.05M
  final lesson       Step (0.6 m/s) -- no earlier generation reached it

For scale, gens 5-11 never exceeded SingleSupportMean 0.031 anywhere, in any
lesson. Gen 11 sat at 0.024 after 12.65M steps in Shuffle alone.

What made the difference was not the reward. Gens 9, 10 and 11 had already
eliminated the reward's shape, gradient and metric by direct measurement. Gen
12 changed the ECONOMICS: a fall stopped ending the episode, so a failed step
cost a 25-step recovery window instead of the whole remainder. Exploring a step
became affordable for the first time.

Gen 12's own limit was the stumble budget, which pinned at 30.00 from 10.25M
onward -- the moment stepping made falls frequent -- putting the survival cliff
back. Gen 13 removes the budget entirely.
