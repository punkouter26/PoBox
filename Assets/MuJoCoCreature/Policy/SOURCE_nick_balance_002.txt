nick_balance_002.onnx
=====================

WHAT      Locomotion brain for Nick (RIGGED_Nick.glb on the MuJoCo Unity plugin),
          trained in MUJOCO WARP -- the second policy in this project not trained
          by Unity ML-Agents, and the comparison point for the Isaac Lab line.
CONTRACT  127 observations / 30 continuous actions.
          CreatureSentisController.GatherObservations with _observeLocomotionCommand
          on: the 121-term balance vector plus commanded speed, pelvis-local
          commanded direction (3) and the 1.4 Hz gait clock (sin, cos).
          Foot contact is rest-relative (ankle within 0.03 m of rest).
          Actions are zero-centred position targets in canonical order.
          Control every 4 physics steps of 0.005 s (decimation 4, 50 Hz).
          The observation normaliser is BAKED INTO THE GRAPH; feed raw.
          input obs_0 [batch,127], output continuous_actions [batch,30].
MODEL     Tools/MuJoCo/nick_unity.xml -- the MJCF MjScene generates, exported by
          RigTool_NickMuJoCo.ExportNickMjcf. NOT the authored creature.xml.
TRAINED   2026-09-08, results/nick/nick08, model_1499.pt (iteration 1499), rsl_rl PPO on mujoco_warp.
          Config: results/nick/nick08/config.json.
MEASURED  BALANCE-ONLY brain for the 0.02 s PhysX contest ring. 95% full-cap at 150 N, 30.0 s median, stance switches 0.087. CANNOT WALK: 0% full-cap, 2.22 s median. Trained at dt=0.02, decimation=1, joint armature 0.2 (10x the MJCF's 0.02).
EXPORT    Tools/MuJoCo/export_onnx.py --run nick08 --checkpoint model_1499.pt
