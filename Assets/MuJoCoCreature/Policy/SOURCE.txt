creature_policy.onnx
====================

Nick balance policy, deployed 2026-08-31.

Provenance
----------
run        PoBox-MuJoCo/runs/nick_unity_v2
checkpoint best.pt, iteration 453 of 600
model      models/nick_unity.xml -- the MJCF that MjScene ACTUALLY generates,
           exported from the Unity scene. NOT the authored models/nick.xml.
           Training against the authored file is what made every earlier
           attempt collapse in Unity.
training   157,286,400 env-steps, 8192 worlds, 200 Hz control, MuJoCo Warp
contract   121 observations / 30 actions, PoBox canonical joint order

Measured (512 fresh-start rollouts, 90 s cap, 0.90 fall threshold)
------------------------------------------------------------------
median survival   10.92 s
mean              12.20 s
max               89.99 s
p25               10.08 s
uprightness       0.983
zero-action baseline  1.39 s

Why this checkpoint and not a later one
---------------------------------------
v3 (sharper height kernel -60, drift weight 0.50, fall threshold 0.90)
regressed hard: median 1.39 s, i.e. no better than no policy at all. Changing
the height kernel and the termination threshold together almost certainly
killed episodes during the normal startup transient, so the policy never
survived long enough to learn what came after. Change one at a time.

v1 measures 0.83 s on the same footing -- worse than doing nothing. The single
biggest win in this line was recovering MuJoCo's exact per-joint kv from
dampratio="1" (read -actuator_biasprm[:,2]) instead of approximating it as
0.1*kp; the true ratios span 0.021 to 0.225. That alone took the median from
0.83 s to 10.92 s.

Known remaining flaw
--------------------
Fails by a slow crouch, not a topple: head holds 1.389 m for ~9 s, sags to
1.346, then goes unstable at ~10 s. 252 of 256 failures are this mode. It also
drifts about 1 m/s -- the drift penalty at weight 0.05 was far too weak, and
raising it to 0.50 was part of what broke v3.

Do not trust the training log
-----------------------------
In-training metrics disagreed with fresh-start evaluation by up to 100x on this
line, because training measures a steady-state population dominated by
survivors while evaluation starts every episode cold. The only measurement that
never contradicted itself is a deterministic fresh-start rollout logging head
height against time.
