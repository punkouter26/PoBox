1. The punch card, and the ringcraft calls

AFTER 1 2 3
  1. Punch card (UI/PunchCard.cs). After a clean punch of 11 N s or more (at most every 12 s), the half second before it is read back from MuJoCo: the peak speed of the hip, shoulder, elbow and glove (mj_objectVelocity, MjBoxer.Chain) and how long before the landing each came. This one is a real Zombie right cross: glove 8.4 m/s, but the shoulder peaked before the elbow and slower than the hip, so it reads ARMS ONLY. Gives way to the brain cam, the replay and the results.
  2. Under it: glove speed in km/h, the impulse, and the floor's hardest push through both feet in body weights (the solver's contact forces on the foot boxes).
  3. Ringcraft calls (Broadcast/Commentary.cs): "X has Y trapped on the ropes" after 2 s on the ropes under pressure (the HUD's ON THE ROPES rule, now Fighter.OnTheRopes), and "They're tied up" after 1.5 s chest to chest.

The shot was held: when the card appeared during a live bout the game was paused (timeScale 0) and the card's timer pinned, because the dev capture takes longer than the card's 4 s. The numbers on it are the live ones.
