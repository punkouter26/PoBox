PoBox: choosing the boxers

1 October 2026 · the interface before and after, with what changed marked on the pictures

Three things were asked for: take slow motion and replays out of the game, take out the glow that lit a joint up when it was working hard, and add a first screen where the two boxers are chosen. Every "before" picture below was taken that afternoon, just before the change; every "after" picture just after it. Letters mark what went, numbers mark what is there now.

The new first screen

NEW · the app now starts here

WHAT IS ON IT
  1. One column for each corner. Every trained boxer has a card in both.
  2. The boxer picked for a corner: full brightness, with a border in the corner's colour.
  3. A boxer not picked for that corner: dimmed. Tap it to pick it.
  4. Name, height and weight, read from the body the boxer was trained in. The picture is the boxer itself, photographed in the ring with its gloves off.
  5. The pairing as it stands, each name in its corner's colour.
  6. FIGHT loads the ring with those two.

The two corners always hold different boxers. Tapping the boxer who is already in the other corner swaps the two over, so with Matt and Zombie the choice is who is red and who is blue. The last pair fought is remembered for next time.

Why a boxer cannot fight a copy of itself. It was built and tried. Two Zombies fight properly. Two Matts do not: each waits for the other to come to it, the two settle into a perfectly matched stand-off, and in 90 seconds of bout time neither threw a punch. Rather than offer a pairing that does nothing, the menu keeps the corners different.

  1. Nothing is drawn over the fighters but their gloves. The glow, the shapes that carried it and the shader code for it are gone.
  2. The scoreboard is unchanged. Joint stress is still measured and can still be read as a number here (tap this row) and in the commentary line; only the light is gone.

The result card: no replay

BEFORE
A B C D E F
  1. The picture was a slow-motion highlight reel of the bout's three best hits, on a loop.
  2. A marker on the momentum graph for each of those hits.
  3. Which clip was playing, and what it was.
  4. A scrubber to drag through the clip by hand.
  5. Previous clip, pause, next clip.
  6. NEXT BOUT, counting down.
AFTER
1 2 3 4
  1. The picture is the ring as the bout ended. Nothing is replayed.
  2. The momentum graph of the bout, with no markers on it.
  3. BOXERS: back to the first screen to choose another pair.
  4. REMATCH: the same two again, counting down to doing it by itself. (It said NEXT BOUT; with the boxers chosen by hand, the next bout is a rematch.)

The card is 110 reference pixels shorter, so more of the ring shows above it. Between rounds, where a replay of the round's best hit used to play for up to nine seconds, there is now a three-second pause.

The menu, MATCH tab: no slow speeds, and a way back

BEFORE
A B C
  1. 0.25x: the whole fight at quarter speed.
  2. 0.5x: half speed.
  3. The picture itself, caught mid slow motion: the close-up, the blurred crowd and the soft focus that came on after a heavy hit.
AFTER
1 2
  1. SPEED is normal or double. Nothing in the game runs slower than real time any more, by hand or by itself.
  2. CHANGE BOXERS: leaves the bout and goes back to the first screen.
