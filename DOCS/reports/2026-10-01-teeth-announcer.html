PoBox: the teeth are the ring announcer

1 October 2026 · the arena before and after, with what changed marked on the pictures

This page shows the announcer as it was first built. On 2 October the owner chose a different behaviour: the teeth appear only for the boxers' introductions and the winner, and are hauled away in between. See 2026-10-02-teeth-announcer-intros.html.

The owner asked for the teeth (2025_TeethUpper.ply and 2025_TeethLower.ply, two dental scans) to be the boxing ring's announcer. They now hang over the middle of the ring on a cable, where an announcer's microphone hangs, and they talk: the jaw opens and shuts with what is being said.

What it says, and when

WhenAloud
The boxers are shown in their corners"In the red corner! MATT! And in the blue corner! ZOMBIE!"

The bout ends"And the winner is! ZOMBIE!", or "It is a draw!"
Any other line of commentary (the text at the bottom)Nothing: it mouths the line, a syllable at a time, for as long as the line would take to say

The voice is the speech synthesiser that comes with Windows (the voice called David), recorded once into fifteen short files: eight phrases and a name for each of the seven boxers, the five now in training included. While a recording plays, the jaw follows its loudness.

Where it is not seen

AFTER · a close shot

The cameras cut close when punches land, and a close shot has no room above the boxers' heads: the teeth are out of the picture, as here. They are seen in the wide shots, at the walk-on and at the result, which is when an announcer speaks.

Not checked: nobody has listened to it. The recordings exist and the game plays them (the jaw was open and moving in play), but what it sounds like through speakers, and how loud it is beside the crowd, has to be judged by ear.

How the scans became a model

The scans arrive in the scanner's own position, in millimetres, 320,000 and 382,000 triangles. A script (training/tools/teeth_to_glb.py, run by Blender) works out which way they face from their shape: up is the thin direction, left to right is the direction the arch mirrors itself in, and the front is the closed end of the U. It stands them upright, thins each to 30,000 triangles, and puts the origin of both where a jaw's hinge is (the red cross: behind and above the back teeth), so the lower jaw opens by turning about one axis.
