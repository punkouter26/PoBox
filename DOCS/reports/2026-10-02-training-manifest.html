PoBox: training manifest

2 October 2026, 00:20 · the plan for "train all boxers until they can do all needed behaviours" · written before any of it was started

In short. Seven boxers. Two (Matt, Zombie) can box and one of them guards; five (Trump, Grandma, Grandpa, Nick, Lil Matt) are half way through learning to box in an eight-hour league that ends about 07:05. Nobody can get up after a knockdown, and the game knocks somebody down about once a round. So the work is: let the league finish, then teach all seven to get up, teach Matt to guard, and put every boxer through one exam that says, behaviour by behaviour, whether it can do what a bout needs.

This project does not use ML-Agents. The procedure asked for (audit, biomechanics, hardware, manifest, then train) is followed with this project's own parts: the trainer is PPO on MuJoCo Warp (training/train_box.py), as the house rules require, and the game steps the same bodies in MuJoCo. Where the procedure says "behaviour parameters" read models/NAME_policy_config.json; "decision requester" is the control period; "mlagents-learn" is train_box.py.

1. Audit: what a policy sees, does and is paid for

PartWhat it isFinding
Observation100 numbers per boxer, in its own heading frame: trunk velocity and tilt (9), joint angles, joint speeds and last action (63), feet on the canvas and pelvis height (3), the opponent's head, body and head velocity (9), own and opponent's gloves (12), opponent's facing and place in the ring (4). Sensor noise in training.The same for every boxer and every stage, so one stage's policy starts the next. Nothing tells a boxer how dazed it is: it can only feel its drives weaken.
Action21 joint targets, guard pose plus action times 0.5 rad, clipped to 3 and to the joint's range. No action masking: every joint is always driven.Reach is 1.5 rad either side of the guard. Enough for a punch; for getting up off the floor it has not been tried.
Decision period50 Hz (every 4 physics steps of 5 ms).Same in the game.
NetworkGaussian policy and critic, one pair per boxer; exported to ONNX with two outputs, actions and value.Fine.
Boxing reward23 terms: standing and facing, range, landing a punch (by zone, hand, power and pace, priced by each boxer's style), being hit, stopping a punch, a knockdown, energy, joint speed and range limits, feet, not leaning on the opponent.Being hit only costs something under the daze rule. Matt and Zombie have had 88 minutes of it, the five new boxers will have had about 50.
Get-up reward13 terms: pelvis and head height, upright trunk, standing; once standing, guard pose, stillness, facing the opponent. A helping force on the trunk that fades as the boxer succeeds; one world in ten never gets it.Never trained. The largest unknown in this plan.
Self-playTwo different boxers in one ring each learn with their own policy; a veteran can be frozen as a sparring partner.Matt's and Zombie's punches settled into a rut: all of Zombie's go to the head, all of Matt's to the body.

From training/tools/audit_bodies.py (new). Every body is 17 simple shapes inside its mesh, the joint ranges are one human template (knee 0 to 150°, elbow 0 to 150°, hip flexion 120°), gravity is Earth's, and peak torques sit at a trained adult's times each boxer's strength.

Two findings. (1) Grandma and Grandpa need about nine tenths of all their knees can give just to hold a deep squat. They can rise from the canvas only with momentum or their arms, as a frail person does, so their get-up is marked more gently and may take the longest to train. This is the owner's choice of age-realistic strength, not a fault. (2) The trunk and hip drives are soft and under-damped (about 1 to 2 Hz, damping ratio 0.2 to 0.4). It makes a body that sways when hit, which looks right, and every policy so far was trained on it; changing it would throw all of them away. Left as it is.

4. The exam: what "can do all needed behaviours" means

One tool, training/tools/exam.py, in plain C MuJoCo (the library the game runs), in the game's ring, without exploration noise. A boxer passes when every line that applies to it passes against each opponent it is tested with.

BehaviourTestPass mark
FootingRounds of 45 s, nothing weakening itfalls by itself no more than once in five minutes
AttackThe same roundslands a punch at least every two seconds, at 4 m/s or more times its own speed factor
GuardPunches that come at its head at over 2 m/sat least three in ten stopped on a glove or forearm, or made to miss. Applies to the boxers whose style pays for a guard (Matt, Zombie, Grandma, Grandpa, Lil Matt); reported for Trump and Nick, who are built without one
ChinThe same rounds under the daze ruleloses its legs no more than three times in two minutes
Getting upA knockdown as the game deals one: a shove and two seconds of slack legs, flat on the canvason its feet unaided inside the count: 8 in 10 within 8 s (Grandma, Grandpa: 6 in 10 within 9 s)
Carrying onThe match policy takes over from where the get-up left the bodystill on its feet 3 s later, 9 times in 10

The marks are set before the results are known and may turn out to be badly placed; if one is moved, the report will say which and why.

Wall clock. The league to about 07:05, then 6 to 11 hours of GPU for the rest: done between early and late afternoon on 2 October if get-up trains as hoped. Get-up is the item that can overrun; it is run first, on the two veterans, and watched for its first twenty minutes before the others are queued.

House rules for each run. TensorBoard is up on localhost:6006; runs nobody compares against are moved to logs/tb_archive; MuJoCo's viewer is opened on each run; self-collision is checked for a boxer before its first run of mine; each run over 30 minutes gets its three charts at three levels in the closing report.
