To measure it yourself

Open Assets/Scenes/Testbed.unity, press Play and read the physics and policy milliseconds on the HUD. In the arena, open the debug panel. Never judge frame rate in an unbaked arena: its six house lights are then live.

If a phone runs slow

The brain is the first thing to look at: 100 calls a second on the CPU. The lever is the 30 FPS option and the render-scale governor; the physics and decision rates must stay at 200 and 50 or the boxers fall.
