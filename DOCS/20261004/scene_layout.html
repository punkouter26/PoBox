Arena Layout

The game has three scenes. The menu picks two boxers, the arena is the ring they fight in, and the testbed is a bare floor where one boxer is checked alone. Every measurement below is read from the scene files and the physics model.

Tier 1 · Quick look

The ring, from above

Ring

6.1 m square inside the ropes. The physics ropes are four solid walls 1.5 m high. The four ropes you see (at 0.45, 0.75, 1.05 and 1.35 m) are drawn only and bow when a boxer leans on the wall.

Spawn points

Walk-on spots 6.1 m apart, then start marks 2.04 m apart on the diagonal, facing each other. After a knockdown with no get-up brain, a boxer is stood up at least 1.3 m from the other.

No obstacles, no goals

The ring is empty. The only target is the other boxer's head and body. The arena holds eight 1 kg cubes parked 40 m away; only the testbed throws them.

There are no Unity trigger zones. Hits, blocks, footsteps and landings are all worked out from MuJoCo's positions and contacts once per brain decision (50 times a second) by MjRing.

Tier 3 · Setup guide

Engine setup

Layers and tags

None to set. The project has no custom tags or layers, and the three scenes hold no Collider, Rigidbody or Joint. Unity physics is set to Script mode and never stepped. A test (TheTestbedHasNoUnityPhysicsInIt) fails if any appear.

Collision groups (MuJoCo)

GroupWhoTouches
1A standing boxerThe other boxer, floor, ropes, cubes
2A downed boxer (ghost)Floor and ropes only
3Floor, ropes, cubesEverything

A boxer that gets up stays a ghost until the two are 0.6 m apart or 3 s pass, so nobody trips on it.

Self-collision

Each boxer has 17 simple shapes (capsules, boxes, spheres) fitted inside its mesh. All pairs collide except the 11 pairs joined by a joint.

Navigation mesh

Not used. Boxers walk by balance and joint drives, not by pathfinding. The ring position (two numbers, as a share of the 3.05 m half-width) is part of what each brain sees.

Time

Fixed timestep is forced to 0.005 s at start (MjRing.Awake, Bout). The project file still says 0.02; leave it. Speed-up changes Time.timeScale (1 to 4), never the step.

Scenes in the build

Assets/Scenes/Menu.unity then Assets/Scenes/Arena.unity. Testbed.unity is editor only. The arena holds each boxer twice (red kit and blue kit), all switched off; MatchSetup switches on the picked pair before MuJoCo compiles.
