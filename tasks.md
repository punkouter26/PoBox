## What was agreed

- Retrofit the boxing game: all seven boxers (Matt, Zombie, Nick, Lil Matt, Trump, Grandma, Grandpa).
- Trainer: MuJoCo Warp 3.14.0 in `training/` (already the latest release).
- Unity: the official `org.mujoco` plugin at **3.5.0** with `bin.mujoco` 3.5.0 (Windows and Android). No PhysX
  component on boxers, ring, ropes or props. Policies run in Sentis from ONNX (opset 17, batch 1).
- Reference runs and the exam use plain C MuJoCo **3.5.0**, so Unity is compared like for like.
- Bodies: all seven re-derived from their meshes with shapes fitted inside the mesh. 12 links, 21 hinge joints,
  PD position targets at 50 Hz, solver step 0.005 s (decimation 4).
- Existing policies are warm starts, not kept as they are.
- Training adds +/-15% randomisation (mass, friction, contact softness, joint gains), shoves and cube impacts.
- Cube pool: 8 cubes, 0.2 m, 1 kg, free joints, parked 40 m away; the same 8 in every training model.
- Walk and turn use free CMU motion-capture clips as a style reward. Everything else is reward-only.
- Observation: today's 100 numbers plus a 3-number walk command; networks widened with zero weights.

## Rules while working

- **Parity rule:** if a boxer stands in C MuJoCo and falls in Unity, stop training and fix the cause first.
- One GPU job at a time. Do not start one while another session's run is on the GPU.
- Every training run: clear obsolete TensorBoard runs, start TensorBoard and the viewer, log the run and the
  decision in `rl_optimization_log.md`. Runs over 30 minutes: close the Unity editor, and write the HTML chart
  report (three charts, three reading levels) that `AGENTS.md` asks for.
- Scenes and prefabs are authored in the editor through the Unity CLI or MCP, not generated by code.
- A UI change gets an annotated before/after screenshot in an HTML report.

## Ladder and pass marks

10 seeds each; the grandparents' marks are scaled to their strength.

| Rung | Behaviour | Pass mark |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | Stand | upright 20 s under 10-30 N s shoves and 1 kg cubes at 5 m/s, 95% of episodes |
| R1 | Walk | tracks 0.3-1.0 m/s within 0.15 m/s over 10 s; at most 0.2 falls a minute |
| Gate | Unity parity | replay output difference under 1e-4; 5 s closed loop: upright in both, cadence within 10%, RMS torque within 15%; fall rate within 0.05 of C MuJoCo over 25 episodes |
| R2 | Turn | opponent anywhere in 360 degrees; faces within 15 degrees inside 3 s, 90% |
| R3 | Get up | standing unaided 1.5 s within 8 s, 80% (60% within 9 s for the grandparents) |
| R4 | Attack | at least 0.5 punches landed a second at 4 m/s times the boxer's speed factor |
| R5 | Guard | stops at least 30% of head shots, or takes under 0.3 a second |
| R6 | Chin | legs taken at most 1.5 times a minute under the daze rule |
| R7 | Carrying on, footing | up 3 s after a get-up, 90%; at most 0.2 unprovoked falls a minute against all six opponents |

Every rung also has to keep each joint's 99th-percentile speed under its human limit.
